## Sub-blueprint

The construction has two genuine stages, in manuscript order:

1. `N04bFieldRealization` retains a cumulative N01--N03 witness and constructs
   actual smooth, periodic corrected coefficients with strict physical type.
   It contains no canonical coordinate or transmission hypothesis.
2. `N04bCanonicalConstruction` constructs the moving physical halves,
   genuine closed inverse charts, height gauge and actual operator identities
   from that raw corrected field.

Their tasks are `N04b-FieldRealization.md` and
`N04b-CanonicalConstruction.md`. The parent theorem assembles these statements
without a local placeholder. The endpoint remains open until both stages
are proved and its transitive axiom audit passes.

The scope decision uses measured source dependencies: the existing optical
source-realization route at `line/N02N03` commit
`b2eee1e4b94012e210e91dee039387e64a88df0a` has 132 changed or new dependency
modules totaling 23,222 Lean lines relative to main `63a4cb2`; it still lacks
the complete source tame tower and a global strict-type bridge. The separate
coordinate-flow and root construction is also substantial (149 related
modules, 20,352 lines absent from main, were inventoried in PR #18).
These are inventories of existing related code, not claims that every line
is uniquely necessary or that their totals can be added without overlap.
They support a construction expected to go well beyond 30,000 lines, so the
repository's sub-blueprint rule applies.
